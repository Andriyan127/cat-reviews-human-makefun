# Judging Checklist — Cat Reviews Its Human

Rubric extracted from the competition rules (judge brief, in order of importance).
Each criterion scored 1–5; maximum 25.

## The rubric

1. **On subject and funny** — a funny one-star review written by a house cat about its human owner. A line off that subject loses to any funny one-star cat review of its human.
2. **Specific, recognisable complaint** — the food bowl, the closed door, the laptop keyboard, rather than vague grumpiness.
3. **Unmistakably feline voice** — the human is measured by cat standards, and the review gives away more about the cat than the owner.
4. **Tight and surprising** — a turn or a sting in the last few words that rewards a second read.
5. **Deadpan review shape, absurd content** — keeps the deadpan shape of a real one-star review while the content goes somewhere absurd.

Notes from the brief: a pun is welcome when it serves the complaint; pun stacking for its own sake does not count. The judge compares two lines at a time, goes through the criteria in order, and lets the first clear gap decide unless a later one differs by much more. There is no tie.

## Bar to beat

No past winners or live leaderboard were visible for this mission (6 submissions so far, blind rounds in progress), so the bar was set from the brief itself: the winning line must score near-perfect on all five criteria, with the deciding edge on criterion 4 (the final sting), since most competent entries will be on-subject and specific.
